    IQL¶

    Implicit Q-Learning learns from a fixed dataset without ever asking its critic about actions the data does not contain, which offline RL's critics overrate. A state value V(s) is fitted as an upper expectile of the critic over the dataset's own actions, which approximates the best of them without a max. The critic then regresses on r + γ V(s'). The actor copies the dataset's actions, each weighted by how much better than V it was (advantage-weighted regression).

    Paper: Kostrikov, Nair and Levine, Offline Reinforcement Learning with Implicit Q-Learning, ICLR 2022 (arXiv:2110.06169). The defaults and the update order are the authors' JAX code, ikostrikov/implicit_q_learning at 09d7002 (learner.py, critic.py, actor.py, policy.py, configs/).

    Can I use?¶

    Feature IQL
    Continuous actions yes; the actor's mean is tanh-bounded
    Discrete actions no
    Offline training yes, train_offline(dataset, n_updates)
    Online fine-tuning yes, train(initial_state=...) after it, the same update
    Memory (memory=) no
    Environments gymnax, Brax, MuJoCo Playground
    Several seeds in one call yes, train(seed=[...])
    Extension phases pretrain, post_update, eval_metrics, observe, on_target, critic_loss, actor_loss, transform_action, actuate, choose_warmup_action, choose_replay_action

    Example¶

    import jax
import jax.numpy as jnp

from ajax import IQL
from ajax.buffers.dataset import record
from ajax.logging.wandb_logging import LoggingConfig


def pd_expert(obs):
    theta = jnp.arctan2(obs[..., 1], obs[..., 0])
    return jnp.clip(-(2.0 * theta + 0.5 * obs[..., 2]), -2.0, 2.0)[..., None]


agent = IQL("Pendulum-v1", buffer_size=10_000)
data = record(pd_expert, agent.env_args, jax.random.PRNGKey(0), n_steps=2_000)
logging = LoggingConfig(config={}, log_frequency=1_000, use_wandb=False)
state, offline = agent.train_offline(data, n_updates=3_000, seed=[0], logging_config=logging)
print(offline["Eval/episodic mean reward"])
state, online = agent.train(seed=[0], n_timesteps=1_000, initial_state=state)

    Evaluations log, next to the return, the losses of the value, the actor and the critics (value/value_loss, value/actor_loss, value/critic_loss), the mean advantage q - V(s) on the batch (value/advantage) and the mean advantage weight (value/weight).

    Key hyperparameters¶

    Argument Default What it does
    expectile 0.7 the expectile V fits (0.5: the mean; towards 1: the best action in the data). The reference uses 0.7 on locomotion, 0.9 on antmaze
    temperature 3.0 the advantage weights' inverse temperature, min(exp(temperature (q - V)), 100); 10 on antmaze
    gamma, tau 0.99, 0.005 discount; Polyak rate of the target critics
    actor_learning_rate, critic_learning_rate, value_learning_rate 3e-4 Adam step sizes (eps 1e-8, optax's)
    actor_architecture, critic_architecture ("256", "relu", "256", "relu") hidden layers, also the value's
    batch_size, buffer_size 256, 1,000,000 replay batch; capacity, which must hold the dataset
    reward_scale 1.0 multiplies the rewards in the critic's target
    learning_starts 0 online: steps before the first update (the replay holds the dataset already)
    normalize_observations False running observation normalisation of the actor and the critics
    expert_policy None an expert the collector tracks (online): its actions feed the observation features and a residual policy (ResidualPolicy), and the evaluation reports its return next to the agent's
    extensions () research features (see Extensions)

    How it works¶

    Each update samples one replay batch and runs, in the reference's order:

    1. Value. V(s) regresses on q = min_i Q_target_i(s, a) at the batch's own actions, by the expectile loss |expectile − 1(q < V)| (q − V)².
    2. Actor. It maximises E[w log π(a|s)] with w = min(exp(temperature (q − V(s))), 100), with V the value just updated. The policy is a Gaussian on a tanh mean, with one learned log-std per action dimension, clipped to [−5, 2]. The evaluations act with its mean.
    3. Critics. Two Q critics regress on reward_scale · r + γ (1 − terminated) V(s'), their squared errors summed.
    4. Targets. The target critics move by Polyak averaging at rate tau.

    Notes¶

    • Learning-rate schedule. For offline training the reference decays the actor's learning rate to 0 by a cosine over its updates. For that, pass actor_learning_rate=optax.cosine_decay_schedule(3e-4, n_updates). Fine-tuning runs keep it constant, as here by default.
    • Rewards. The reference scales a locomotion dataset's rewards by 1000 over its range of episode returns, and subtracts 1 from antmaze's. Compute the factor from your dataset and pass it as reward_scale (or shift the dataset's rewards).
    • The value's input. V reads the raw observation. With normalize_observations=True only the actor and the critics normalise theirs.

    Results¶

    No learning curves are published for IQL yet. Unit tests (tests/agents/IQL) check the expectile loss and the reference's defaults. The probe (tests/probing/test_offline.py) trains IQL offline on logged one-step rows: V must reach the expectile of their returns, and the actor the advantage-weighted mean of their actions, with the weights clipped.

    Bases: PolicyUpdateStart, GradientClipping, ObservationNormalization, ExpertGuided, ReplayAgent

    Implicit Q-Learning: a state value fitted as an expectile of the critic over the data's actions, and an actor fitted by advantage-weighted regression (the module's equations). Train it on a dataset with train_offline (the paper's setting), then fine-tune online with train: the same update, the offline rows kept in its replay (Sec. 5.3).

    Defaults are the reference code's (configs/mujoco_config.py, learner.py, policy.py): expectile 0.7 and temperature 3 (locomotion; antmaze takes 0.9 and 10); 256-256 ReLU networks, orthogonal(sqrt 2) initialisation; Adam at 3e-4 with optax's eps 1e-8 for the actor, the critics and the value; the actor a Gaussian on a tanh mean with one learned log-std per action dimension, clipped to [-5, 2]; batch 256, gamma 0.99, tau 0.005. The reference decays the actor's offline learning rate to 0 by a cosine over its updates: pass actor_learning_rate=optax.cosine_decay_schedule(3e-4, n_updates) for that. Its locomotion rewards are scaled by 1000 over the dataset's range of returns: reward_scale.
