    Agent benchmark¶

    Ten agents on nineteen tasks, with 10 seeds each and their default settings apart from n_envs, which follows each agent's paper. How Ajax's agents compare with other implementations is in the results.

    Setup¶

    • Tasks. Fifteen DeepMind Control tasks on MuJoCo Playground, trained for 1,048,576 steps (APO 1,024,000, a whole number of its rollouts), and four MinAtar games on gymnax, trained for 5,242,880 steps.
    • Machines. CPU only, on 4-core cloud machines, seeds 0 to 9 trained together. DQN on Freeway and SpaceInvaders ran in two halves, for memory.
    • Code. The main branch at acd1626, after 0.5.0.
    • Score. The return at the last evaluation, as mean ± standard deviation over the seeds, with the best mean in each row in bold. With 10 seeds, a small gap between two agents means little. DeepMind Control scores run from 0 to 1000.
    • Records. benchmarks/suite holds the driver and each run's records.

    Reading the tables¶

    • APO on HumanoidWalk is unfinished. Its records stop at step 81,920 of 1,024,000, because the next chunk hit non-finite values twice, so its score comes from early in training. The likely source is the simulator.
    • Two runs are second attempts, from step 0. PPO's first attempt on HumanoidWalk also hit non-finite values, past step 327,680, and AVG's on HopperStand was cut off by a machine restart.
    • No agent learned HopperHop at this budget.
    • The last evaluation can swing. On BallInCup, 6 of PPO's seeds ended above 890 and 4 at 0, and APO ended at 0 after scoring higher earlier.
    • Hopper and Finger runs turned an XLA pass off, as Environments explains.

    Agents not in the tables¶

    REDQ, TQC, CrossQ, DreamerV3 and TD-MPC2 were timed and left out, as they are too slow to train 10 seeds this way on a CPU. APG needs a differentiable task, IQL a dataset and DAgger an expert to query, and TD-MPC2's multi-task agent trains on a suite of tasks.
